Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

fun_faicts publishes short fun-fact videos. The fun_faicts publishing app is used only by the fun_faicts team to upload our own videos to our own accounts on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok.

What we collect

The app does not collect, store or share personal data of viewers or other users. It only uses access to the fun_faicts accounts to upload videos and to read the view statistics of our own posts.

Google and YouTube data

The app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the fun_faicts YouTube channel and read its statistics. It does not access any other Google user's data. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can also read the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are used only to publish fun_faicts content. You can revoke the app's access at any time in your Google, Meta or TikTok account settings.

Contact

Questions? Send a direct message to fun_faicts on Instagram.
